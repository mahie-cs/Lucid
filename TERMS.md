## 6. Your information

I don't run any server that collects your information, and Lucid has no ads or analytics.

- **On your phone.** Your library, downloads, settings, search history and the session that keeps you
  signed in are stored on your device. They stay there unless a feature below sends them out.
  Signing out, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Lucid removes them.
- **Sent to services when you use them.** Signing in and playing music sends the usual requests to
  Google and YouTube. Lyrics lookups send a song's title, artist and length to the lyrics
  providers. Listen Together
  sends your display name, a room code and what is playing to its relay and the people in your room.
- **Crash reports.** Lucid has the means to send crash reports, but builds made from this repository
  have no reporting address set, so none are sent.
- **Backups.** Backup files you make are saved where you choose. They hold your library and
  settings, so keep them private.

The services above handle your information under their own policies.
